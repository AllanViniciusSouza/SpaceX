Test day checklist
  • Before login: link IO_VFDs.VTD_WcState and IO_VFDs.VTD_EcState in XAE, then Activate Configuration.
  • Read-only first: compare items 5–11 with MOVISUITE (Startup › process data monitor). Counter 6 must rise; position 9 must follow the carriage.
  • Target position for PO 7/8: high word = position DIV 65536, low word = position MOD 65536 (positive values). Example 100000 → PO 7 = 1, PO 8 = 34464 (16#86A0).
  • Do not enable item 13 (control word) before SEW confirms the MOVIKIT Positioning Drive control word bits. Vertical axis: verify brake and stop behaviour first.
